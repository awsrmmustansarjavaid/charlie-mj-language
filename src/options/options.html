Subtitle & timeline engine

Charlie MJ reads YouTube caption tracks directly and synchronizes them to the HTML5 video currentTime. YouTube CC can remain off.

Subtitle display & learning modes

Word-to-word mode keeps source POS colors on the corresponding translated tokens. Sentence-to-sentence mode presents the complete source sentence and its complete translation as synchronized learning lines.

Original subtitle typography

Translation subtitle typography

Translation words inherit the source word's POS color, including nouns, verbs, adjectives and other categories.

Video learning controls

A/B replay, sentence replay, slow playback and transcript timestamp jumping are available from the toolbar.
