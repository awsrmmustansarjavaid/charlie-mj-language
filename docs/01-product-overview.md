# Product Overview

## What is Charlie MJ Language?

Charlie MJ Language is a local-first Chrome extension designed to turn supported online video pages, especially YouTube, into focused language-learning environments.

It combines subtitle capture, transcript management, vocabulary mining, bookmarks, screenshots, focus controls, exports and review-oriented productivity features in one tool.

## Why it was built

The project was inspired by the practical problem of watching foreign-language videos while needing to repeatedly switch between subtitle tools, dictionaries, notes, screenshots and vocabulary systems. The goal is to keep the learner's attention on the video while making useful language data easy to capture.

## Product principle

**Watch → Understand → Capture → Organize → Review → Practice → Remember.**

## Target users

- Beginners through advanced language learners.
- Learners who use YouTube as immersion material.
- Learners who build vocabulary from real sentences.
- Learners who use Anki or other review systems.
- Learners who want portable exports instead of vendor lock-in.

## Design principles

1. Local-first data ownership.
2. Minimal distraction.
3. Keyboard-friendly productivity.
4. Multi-language architecture.
5. Export everything important.
6. External translation and AI are optional.
7. No GitHub Actions or CI service is required to build or load the extension.
